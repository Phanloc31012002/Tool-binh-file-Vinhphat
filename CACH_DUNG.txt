========================================
  PANEL "CONG CU BINH" - CACH CAI
========================================

LAN DAU TIEN (chi lam 1 lan):
  1. Bam dup: bat_debug_mode.bat   -> bat cho phep chay extension
  2. Bam dup: install.bat          -> copy panel vao Illustrator
  3. Mo Illustrator: Window > Extensions > Cong cu binh

CAC LAN CAP NHAT SAU (khi co ban moi):
  - Neu da cau hinh update online: may tu kiem tra luc dang nhap Windows,
    va tu cai ban moi khi Illustrator dang dong.
  - Muon cap nhat ngay: dong HAN Illustrator, mo Start Menu > CongCuBinh va bam
    "Cap nhat Cong cu binh". Khong can khoi dong lai Windows.
  - Neu chua cau hinh online: dong HAN Illustrator truoc, bam dup install.bat.

PHAT HANH BAN MOI (may quan ly):
  - Sua chuc nang can sua trong source.
  - Bam dup PhatHanhCapNhat.bat, nhap mo ta ngan, roi doi script push xong.
  - Khong sua tay version, online\latest.json hay file ZIP.
  - Mo lai Illustrator (chi dong/mo panel khong nap lai script JSX).

LUU Y:
  - Dat 2 file .bat CUNG CHO voi thu muc DanCardCEP.
  - De bat update online: tao GitHub repository Public, sau do chay
    Configure-OnlineUpdate.ps1 mot lan tren may tao ban phat hanh.
  - Neu Windows canh bao "Windows protected your PC" khi chay .bat:
    bam "More info" > "Run anyway" (file an toan, chi copy thu muc).

DAN CATALOGUE A4 / A5:
  1. Trong Illustrator, chon cac trang theo thu tu doc
     (trai -> phai, tren -> duoi).
  2. So trang phai la boi so cua 4.
  3. Mo tab Catalogue, chon Dan Catalogue A4 hoac A5.
  4. Lan dau tien, chon file pon .ai cua dung kho.
